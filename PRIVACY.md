# Privacy Policy

**Last updated: March 25, 2026**

## Overview

HIITime ("the App") is a HIIT workout timer for iPhone and Apple Watch. Your privacy is important to us. This policy explains what data the App collects and how it is used.

## Data Collection

**HIITime does not collect, store, or transmit any personal data.**

- We do not run servers or backend infrastructure.
- We do not use analytics services or third-party SDKs.
- We do not collect any identifiers, usage data, or diagnostic information.

## Data Stored on Your Device

All data created by HIITime (routines, workout history) is stored **locally on your device only**, using Apple's SwiftData framework. This data never leaves your device.

## HealthKit

If you grant permission, HIITime reads and writes workout data (heart rate, active calories, workout sessions) to Apple Health on your device. This data is governed by Apple's HealthKit privacy rules and is never accessed by us.

## Apple Watch

Workout data displayed on your Apple Watch is transferred directly between your iPhone and Watch via Apple's Watch Connectivity framework. It is not routed through any external servers.

## Children's Privacy

HIITime does not knowingly collect any data from anyone, including children under the age of 13.

## Changes to This Policy

If we update this policy, we will update the "Last updated" date above and push the change to this repository.

## Contact

If you have any questions about this privacy policy, please [open an issue](https://github.com/pantry-org/HIITime-Support/issues).
